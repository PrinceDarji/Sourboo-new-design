Sakshi's Diary • Issue 02

HI, I AM SAKSHI. I MAKE SOURBOO.

🥒
sakshi in the kitchen with fresh cucumbers & spices

"I GREW UP OBSESSED WITH KHATTA."

while other kids were buying chocolate bars after school, i was sneaking raw mango slices, imli rolls, and licking chaat masala off my fingers until my tongue burned.

When I grew up, every pickle jar in the supermarket looked like it belonged in the 1970s — drowning in heavy oil, filled with synthetic vinegar, or just plain boring. I wanted a snackable pickle. Something crunchy you could eat straight from the jar with a fork while staring into the abyss of your laptop screen.

"So I bought 10 kilos of baby gherkins, locked myself in my kitchen, and made 40 different batches until I heard that thunderous CRUNCH. That was the birth of Sourboo."
