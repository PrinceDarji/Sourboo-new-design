The current product section has poor alignment. Text is difficult to read, and the CTA buttons do not line up properly.

- Use the approved Sourboo brand colours.
- Do not mix purple and pink within the same visual treatment.
- Ensure text colours have strong contrast against their backgrounds.
- Align both product columns consistently.
- Product descriptions should occupy consistent visual space.
- Align prices and Add to Bag buttons across both products.
- Buttons should have matching sizes, spacing and alignment.
- Ensure responsive layouts remain clean on mobile.
- Do not use unnecessary boxes, cards, borders or rectangular containers.

Do not change product names, prices, descriptions or product functionality.

## 4. Checkout Page: Simplify and Improve

The client dislikes the current checkout experience.

Since this is a checkout page, it should be clean, simple, trustworthy and easy to use.

Redesign the checkout experience with the following goals:

- Remove unnecessary visual clutter.
- Make the checkout process feel clean and familiar.
- Make product details, quantities, prices and totals easy to understand.
- Make payment and delivery steps clear.
- Ensure the layout works well on mobile.
- Do not force the homepage's heavy animations into checkout.
- Do not introduce distracting effects that could reduce checkout completion.
- Keep the checkout functional.

Use the Sourboo brand colours subtly. Prioritise usability over visual gimmicks.

Do not break the existing Shopify checkout, payment, cart or order flow.

Create a custom, playful Thank You page after successful purchase.
